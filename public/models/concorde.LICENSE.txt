Applies to concorde.glb.
Attribution transcribed from the asset.extras metadata embedded in the GLB
(Sketchfab glTF export, 2048x2048 fuselage texture). The GLB is distributed
unchanged.

Runtime adaptations by Flight3d contributors:
- Discard the Sketchfab display rotation and mount the authored 1:1 metre
  geometry in the simulator frame (+X starboard, +Y up, -Z nose).
- Droop the nose section ahead of the windscreen frame about a lateral hinge
  by rotating its vertices at runtime (0, 5 and 12.5 degree positions); the
  original asset has no articulated nose.
- Add procedurally generated landing gear (twin-wheel nose leg, four-wheel
  main bogies); the original asset is modelled gear-up without landing gear.

The livery depicts a real airline's markings as authored in the source model.
Aircraft and airline names are used descriptively; no endorsement by the
original creator, the manufacturers or the airline is implied.
