Modifications by Flight3d contributors, applied at runtime (src/environment/nodeulPavilion.js):
- Scaled 8.5x, rotated 90 degrees about the vertical axis and placed on the
  Nodeulseom island of the Hangang training course.
- The BLEND facade material is rendered as an opaque alpha cut-out (alphaTest 0.5).
- A collision heightfield (src/config/nodeulPavilionHeights.json) is derived from
  its triangles by scripts/build-hangang-assets.mjs.

The model depicts the Sejong Government Complex Cultural Center. Flight3d uses it as
a stand-in pavilion; it does not represent the real Nodeulseom buildings.
