Attribution is also embedded in the GLB scene extras.

Changes by Flight3d contributors (scripts/build-concorde-cockpit.mjs):
- Extracted the flight-deck geometry (seats, instrument panel, pedestal and
  surrounding fittings) from the full aircraft model; the airframe, cabin,
  livery and glass were not retained.
- Scaled to the real 61.66 m aircraft length, narrowed 15%, moved, and
  clipped so it fits inside the manilov.ap Concorde exterior.
- Replaced the materials with per-vertex colours (the carpet texture became a
  flat grey), merged everything into one flat-shaded mesh, and added a
  generated forward bulkhead below the glareshield.
