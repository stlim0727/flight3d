Runtime adaptations by Flight3d contributors:
- Scale the SketchUp-unit geometry to the real 61.66 m length and mount it in
  the simulator frame (+X starboard, +Y up, -Z nose).
- Remove the authored static landing gear and add procedurally generated
  retractable gear (twin-wheel nose leg, four-wheel main bogies).
- Droop the nose section ahead of the windscreen frame about a lateral hinge
  by rotating its vertices at runtime (0, 5 and 12.5 degree positions).
- Reduce the windscreen glass opacity for flight-deck visibility.
