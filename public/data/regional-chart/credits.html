Regional chart data · 지도 자료

World administrative areas, land boundaries and populated places are simplified from Natural Earth 1:10m (public domain).

Nepal province boundaries are adapted from the Survey Department of Nepal (2020), via geoBoundaries gbOpen NPL ADM1 under CC BY 3.0 IGO. Flight3d simplifies, clips and quantizes the geometry, updates province names and adds Korean labels.

Khumbu settlement coordinates come from Wikidata (Lukla, Phakding, Namche Bazaar, CC0).

Water and coastlines use Overture Maps, including © OpenStreetMap contributors. Airport and runway data come from OurAirports.
